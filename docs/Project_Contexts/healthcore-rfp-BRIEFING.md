# BRIEFING — HealthCore: Milestone 9, Agentic Workflow Generation (Parts 1, 2 and 3)

Archived from the syllabus `content/contexts/09-agentic-workflows/healthcore-BRIEFING.md` for this monorepo. Sample PDFs used as upload fixtures (not pre-seeded inventory) are in [`rfp-requests/healthcore/`](./rfp-requests/healthcore/).

> This document applies to all three parts of Milestone 9. Read it in full before starting Part 1 — Parts 2 and 3 reuse the same departments, RFP format, and guidelines defined here.
>
> **Non-negotiable constraint:** HealthCore operates under HIPAA (US) and UK GDPR (UK). No patient identifier or PHI may appear in any event, table, endpoint, log, ticket, or document generated by this flow — not even as an illustrative example.

## 1. Introduction

HealthCore doesn't have a traditional Sales department. Institutional RFPs (occupational health contracts with employers, corporate wellness programs, referral partnerships with universities) land on **Tom Callahan's** desk, the **Revenue Cycle Director**. For this milestone, Revenue Cycle is Sales: they open the ticket and wait for the agentic flow's result.

Putting together an institutional proposal takes about **3 weeks**, coordinated by email between Revenue Cycle, Clinical Operations, and Compliance. Any contract touching patient data has to clear regulatory review before it goes out.

## 2. Departments and data structures

Use exactly these department identifiers:

| `department_id` | Department | Owner | What it contributes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `revenue` | Revenue Cycle | Tom Callahan | Financial terms, currency, payment structure. Owns the ticket. |
| `clinical` | Clinical Operations | Dr. Marcus Reid | Which clinics and staff capacity can cover the contract |
| `compliance` | Compliance and Data Governance | Claire Whitfield | HIPAA / UK GDPR, BAA or DPA depending on the client's country |

`compliance` is mandatory on every RFP.

RFPs arrive as PDFs and typically include: institutional client name and country (US or UK), program type (occupational health, corporate wellness, referral network), volume, deadline, and sometimes a reference budget.

Persist **Ticket**, **RFP metadata**, and **DepartmentSection** (`key_aspects` in Part 1) in PostgreSQL via SQLModel. Never store patient identifiers.

Ticket statuses for Part 1: `analyzing`, `discarded`, `intake_complete`. Later parts add `drafting`, `under_evaluation`, `needs_human_review`, `waiting_for_approval`, and `done`. Part 1 must not use those.

Workers receive shared metadata plus department-relevant extracts. If volume is missing, record an open question. Never invent headcount or PHI.

## 3. Sample uploads

1. `healthcore-request-1.pdf` — formal RFP (accept). Meridian Manufacturing (Austin, 800 employees), on-site occupational health and wellness, 12-month contract. Triggers `revenue`, `clinical`, and `compliance` (BAA). Currency: USD.
2. `healthcore-request-2.pdf` — informal RFP (accept). Thames Valley University email requesting a referral network partnership with a satellite clinic. Triggers `revenue`, `clinical`, and `compliance` (DPA / UK GDPR). Currency: GBP.
3. `healthcore-request-3.pdf` — invalid (reject). Vendor pitch for an EHR system. Classifier must discard it.
4. An RFP that attaches a clinical case with a patient's name and diagnosis must never pass that content into generators, logs, or the ticket UI. Detect, redact, and flag for Compliance review.

## 4. Constraints carried forward

- No generated section may contain patient names, diagnoses, or any patient identifier.
- US clients need a BAA clause. UK clients need a DPA clause referencing UK GDPR. Part 2 and Part 3 enforce this; Part 1 only records country and currency.
- USD for US clients, GBP for UK clients, from `client_country`.
- Compliance approval is always mandatory in Part 3.

## 5. Part 1 deliverable

The ticket identifies whether a document is a HealthCore RFP, extracts metadata without patient data, flags any PHI, and splits the analysis across `revenue`, `clinical`, and `compliance` (compliance always active).
