- Read `track()`, the queue, the 10-second / 20-event flush, `sendBeacon` on `visibilitychange`, and the three-attempt backoff.
- Confirmed telemetry `fetch` and `sendBeacon` exist only in `uis/backoffice/lib/telemetry.ts`.
- Matched the eight mandatory `track()` call sites to the approved plan.
- Posted one envelope to `http://127.0.0.1:3001/hc-api/telemetry/events` after the URL was moved to `NEXT_PUBLIC_TELEMETRY_ENDPOINT`. Response: **200** `{"received":1}`.
